## Image-order bug in the pooled g(n) curve
**Found:** the pooled g(n) curve depended on the order of the images, and that order is random with -j > 1
(`step1_configure` fills `configs` in the order the parallel jobs finish; step 3b passes the images to
`gain_vs_charge.measure` in that order). In the AMtest test EXT1 went from "3.8 % lower at 1000 e-, measured to 480 e-"
to "unreliable curve, no correction".
**Fixed for the default** by commit 9550705 (coherent pooling, `--gain-combine coherent`, the new default):
`_measure_coherent` sorts the images, also for its incoherent fallback. Checked: `measure()` on the AMtest images in the
two orders of the regression gives identical results; two concurrent `-j 6` runs IDENTICAL; 27/27 tests pass.
**Still open (Dylan's decision):** `--gain-combine incoherent` is still order-dependent (g0 from images[0]); making it
order-independent changes incoherent results. Note: the default now changes module curves of multi-image runs
compared with the earlier (incoherent) method.
